Interest Calculation Parameters

Configure client-specific interest rates, day basis, and grace period thresholds.

Grace Period Threshold Configuration
Important Audit Rule: Grace Period is a Threshold, Not a Deduction
The grace period is an ignored delay threshold. If actual delay is within the selected grace period (e.g. 1 day late with 1 day grace), no interest is calculated.
However, if the delay exceeds the grace period (e.g. 3 days late with 1 day grace), interest is calculated on the FULL 3 days, NOT (3 - 1 = 2 days).
% p.a.
Default standard is 12.00% per annum.
Interest Formula: Payment Amount × Rate × Late Days / Day Basis
